# Plan 17-01: Multi-Camera Urban Sensing & Missing Infrastructure Perception

<planning_context>
### Objective
Extend RoadSaathi's edge perception schemas, spatial intelligence engine, and central command visualizers beyond pavement potholes and structural cracks into comprehensive municipal urban sensing in strict alignment with statutory Indian Road Congress codes (IRC:35, IRC:67, IRC:SP:84, IRC:SP:42) and Vision Zero principles:

1. **IRC Standards-Aligned Missing Infrastructure Perception (SENSE-01, D-17-01)**:
   - Detect, classify, and index four critical road infrastructure deficiencies:
     - `MISSING_ROAD_DIVIDER`: Broken, damaged, or absent median barriers under IRC:SP:84 / MoRTH Guidelines posing head-on collision danger.
     - `MISSING_ZEBRA_CROSSING`: Faded, obscured, or absent pedestrian crossing paint under **IRC:35:2015 (Code of Practice for Road Markings)**.
     - `DAMAGED_TRAFFIC_SIGN`: Missing, obscured, or vandalized regulatory, cautionary, and informatory signboards under **IRC:67:2022 (Code of Practice for Road Signs)**.
     - `WATERLOGGING_HAZARD`: Pavement surface water accumulation with estimated depth in centimeters ($d_{water} \in [5, 30]\text{ cm}$), surface area $m^2$, drainage failure index ($DFI = \frac{d_{water} \times A_{surface}}{100}$), and aquaplaning risk index.
   - Extend `DBDistressCluster`, SQLite auto-migration pragmas, and REST endpoints to persist and query these deficiencies.

2. **Vision Zero School Zone Safety Alerting (SENSE-02, D-17-02)**:
   - Proactively guard school zones and vulnerable pedestrians when transit buses equipped with multi-camera sensing enter designated school POI buffers ($d_{poi} < 150\text{ meters}$):
     - Enforces the statutory 20 km/h school zone speed limit (IRC:35 / MoRTH Guidelines).
     - Identifies vulnerable student pedestrians crossing the carriageway.
     - Evaluates crosswalk condition (escalating from P1 to P0 Critical if zebra stripes are missing or faded).
     - Enforces zero-penalty mandate for vulnerable pedestrians ($0.0 fine under Vision Zero; duty of care rests 100% on vehicular drivers).

3. **Hit-and-Run / Rash Driving Real-Time Forensic Alert Dock (SENSE-02, D-17-03)**:
   - Equip `CommandCenter.tsx` with a top high-priority forensic alert dock for fleeing vehicles:
     - High-precision ANPR plate extraction with OCR confidence score ($\ge 90\%$).
     - Synchronized NavIC (IRNSS) satellite GPS coordinates and UTC timestamp.
     - Speed delta ($\Delta v > 15\text{ km/h}$) and impact jerk sensor correlation ($G_z > 2.2g$).
     - 1-click action: **"Dispatch Alert to Traffic Police (GCTP) / Issue Statutory E-Challan"** (MVA 1988 Sec 134/187 or Sec 184).

4. **Edge Bandwidth Minimization & Telemetry Streaming (<2KB/s) (D-17-04)**:
   - Support 5Hz lightweight JSON telemetry (<1.4 KB/s < 2 KB/s constraint) carrying vehicle counts, corridor PCU/km (IRC:106), active camera tags (`CAM-01 Front`, `CAM-02 Rear`, `CAM-03 Left Curb`, `CAM-04 Right Lane`), and NavIC GPS vectors.
   - Restrict forensic keyframe snapshot (200KB JPEG) uploads strictly to confirmed road defect triggers or traffic crime events, achieving $> 95\%$ cellular data bandwidth reduction compared to raw RTSP video streaming.

5. **WebGIS Analytical Layers & Operator Inspector (SENSE-01, SENSE-02)**:
   - WebGIS Map analytical layer toggles: "Missing Road Infrastructure (IRC:35/IRC:67)" and "Vision Zero School Safety Zones".
   - Rich statutory inspections in `OperatorInspectorPanel.tsx` with exact IRC code citations, water depth in cm, and driver yield advisories.

### Threat Model
- **T-17-01: False-Positive Hit-and-Run / ANPR Reads Triggering Erroneous Police Dispatch**:
  - *Risk*: Optical misreads in low-light conditions could extract incorrect plate numbers or misinterpret sharp overtaking as an evasion trajectory, triggering false e-Challans or unjustified police interceptor dispatch.
  - *Mitigation*: Multi-stage validation: kinematic sequence requirement (impact shock $G_z > 2.2g$ or proximity $< 150\text{px}$ followed by speed delta $\Delta v > 15\text{ km/h}$), confidence gating ($\ge 90\%$ ANPR confidence required for auto-admissibility; $< 90\%$ routed to `PENDING_REVIEW` queue), Section 65B BSA cryptographic SHA-256 certificate generation, and role-based access control (`admin`, `safety`) on dispatch endpoints.
- **T-17-02: Cellular Bandwidth Exhaustion on Public Transit Bus Fleets**:
  - *Risk*: Continuous streaming of multi-camera video from 500+ fleet buses over cellular modems exceeds municipal data quotas and incurs unsustainable telecommunications costs.
  - *Mitigation*: Strict architectural constraint (D-17-04): multi-camera inference runs locally on Rockchip RK3588 NPUs; bus streams only lightweight 5Hz JSON metadata ($< 1.4\text{ KB/s} < 2\text{ KB/s}$ limit); 200KB forensic keyframe snapshot uploads strictly on confirmed defect or traffic crime events ($> 95\%$ data reduction).
- **T-17-03: Vision Zero School Zone Misclassification & Penalty Inversion**:
  - *Risk*: Automated traffic systems might penalize child pedestrians crossing midblock or fail to identify vehicles exceeding the statutory 20 km/h speed limit near school entrances.
  - *Mitigation*: Deterministic Vision Zero safety rules: calculate geodesic haversine distance to registered school POIs ($< 150\text{m}$ buffer); enforce statutory 20 km/h threshold; strictly guarantee `fine_amount_inr = 0.0` for pedestrian crossers (eliminating victim blaming); escalate missing zebra crossings to P0 critical municipal repair work orders under IRC:35.
