# Plan 10-01: Tabbed Executive Analytics Redesign, Clean Backlog Table Styling, and Drawer Progressive Disclosure

## Planning Context

### Objective
Transform the heavyweight, un-segmented `Analytics.tsx` (979 LOC) and cluttered `WorkOrders.tsx` (1107 LOC) into sleek, high-density, minimal executive workspaces in accordance with Milestone `v3.2` and Requirement `DECLUTTER-03`:
1. **5-Tab Segmented Analytics Workspace (`Analytics.tsx`):**
   - Eliminate chart overload and 600px+ vertical scrolling by organizing the analytical surfaces into 5 discrete sub-workspaces:
     - 📊 **Overview KPI (`overview`)**: Executive scorecards (`MetricCards`), 4-stat ribbon, Speed vs Distress dual-spline area chart, IRC:SP:20 Statutory RPI Weights radial bar chart, PCI decay bar chart, and MoRTH Liquidated Damages donut chart.
     - 🛣️ **Corridor Health (`corridors`)**: 5-corridor arterial risk ranking matrix, P0/P1/P2 defect breakdown with color-coded progress bars, and Edge fleet ingest diagnostics.
     - 🚦 **Traffic & OD Flow (`traffic`)**: GTFS transit delay attribution ($t_{distress}$ vs $t_{traffic}$), IRC:106 bottleneck choke points & LoS grades, and SIH PS 26124 Origin-Destination (OD) Transit Desire Lines flow matrix.
     - 🛡️ **Safety & Lighting (`safety`)**: Vision Zero safe school & hospital corridor index with institutional letter grades (A+ to F), IRC:35 zebra compliance, and night-time luminescence (<5 Lux) dark spot outage audit table.
     - 👁️ **Edge ANPR & Vision AI (`anpr`)**: Interactive license plate OCR scanner test runner (Section 65B Indian Evidence Act compliant), and arterial PCU traffic density & vehicle classification grid.
   - Implement active indigo pill switchers (`bg-indigo-50 dark:bg-indigo-950/60 text-indigo-700 dark:text-indigo-300 font-semibold border border-indigo-200 dark:border-indigo-800/80 shadow-xs`) with responsive horizontal scrolling.
   - Wire two-way URL query synchronization (`#/analytics?tab=overview | corridors | traffic | safety | anpr`) and configure legacy route aliases (`#/traffic`, `#/pedestrian`, `#/darkspots`, `#/incidents`) in `App.tsx`.

2. **Clean CAD Backlog Table Redesign & Progressive Disclosure (`WorkOrders.tsx`):**
   - Redesign CAD backlog table rows with compact cell density (`py-2.5 px-3`), subtle light/dark borders (`border-slate-200 dark:border-slate-800`), and monospace tabular numbers (`font-mono tabular-nums`).
   - Group fragmented action buttons into 3 high-contrast operational clusters: `Inspect` (3D Mesh / CAD Audit), `Release Escrow` (3-Pass Concurrence Gate), and `Dispatch` (WhatsApp/SMS Ward Notice).
   - Ensure seamless trigger of `WorkOrderActionConsole.tsx` slide-over drawer for progressive disclosure during deep forensic audits.
   - Provide multi-column sorting (Ticket ID, Statutory RPI, Road Corridor, Estimated Cost, SLA Countdown), filter chips (P0/P1/P2, Agency, SLA State), and 1-click RFC 4180 CSV export.

### Scope Boundaries
- **In Scope**: `Analytics.tsx`, `WorkOrders.tsx`, and `App.tsx` routing aliases.
- **Out of Scope**: No backend API modifications; maintain full backward compatibility with the existing 34 backend automated regression test suite.

### Threat Model
- **T-10-01: Route Breakage & Bookmark Invalidation**:
  - *Risk*: Users or external links navigating to legacy routes (`#/traffic`, `#/pedestrian`, `#/darkspots`, `#/incidents`) receive 404 or blank views.
  - *Mitigation*: Update `App.tsx` route handler to intercept these legacy aliases and rewrite them to `#/analytics?tab=<tab_name>` seamlessly with full query preservation.
- **T-10-02: State Loss on Tab Navigation**:
  - *Risk*: Switching between Analytics tabs re-fetches telemetry or loses live ANPR OCR test state.
  - *Mitigation*: Maintain top-level state in `Analytics.tsx` for fetched datasets (`safeCorridors`, `darkSpots`, `transitDelays`, `bottlenecks`, `trafficDensity`, `odData`, `anprResult`) and render conditionally based on `activeTab` without unmounting state holders.
- **T-10-03: Performance / Memory Regression on Simultaneous Chart Rendering**:
  - *Risk*: Multiple hidden ApexCharts instances consuming GPU and CPU threads in the background.
  - *Mitigation*: Conditional tab rendering ensures only the active sub-tab's charts and tables are mounted and rendered in the DOM.
