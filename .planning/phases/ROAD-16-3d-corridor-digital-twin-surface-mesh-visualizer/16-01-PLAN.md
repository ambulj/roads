# Plan 16-01: 3D Corridor Digital Twin & Surface Mesh Visualizer (Concept Prototype)

<planning_context>
### Objective
Equip municipal engineers, PWD executive engineers, and highway maintenance auditors with an interactive 3D road surface elevation & cavity depth mesh explorer to visually inspect pavement structural distress topology, simulate cross-sectional depth slicing (0–25 cm), and switch between multi-camera exterior bus perspectives (Front Windshield, Rear Road Surface, Left Curb/Footpath, Right Carriageway) with synchronized telemetry HUD strips, fulfilling Milestone `v5.0` and Requirements `TWIN-01` and `TWIN-02`:

1. **Persistent Concept Disclaimer Banner & Warning Badge (TWIN-01, D-16-01)**:
   - Render a persistent, non-dismissible amber/indigo concept banner across the top of `RoadMeshVisualizerModal.tsx`:
     *"⚠️ Experimental Concept / Proof-of-Concept Prototype: Pavement 3D surface mesh synthesized from monocular dashcam depth + IMU elevation profile (Non-LiDAR exploratory prototype)"*.
   - Provide an informative hoverable derivation tooltip explaining that the 3D elevation topology is generated via optical disparity inference from monocular dashcam frames cross-correlated with speed-normalized vertical axle acceleration ($G_{norm} = \frac{\Delta G_z}{v / 30}$), not millimeter-grade mobile LiDAR survey hardware.
   - Display explicit `Concept Prototype` / `(Concept)` badges across the modal title bar and all entry point triggers.

2. **3D Surface Mesh & Depth Slicing Modes (TWIN-01, D-16-02)**:
   - Implement an interactive depth cut-plane slider ($0\text{ cm}$ at surface grade down to $25\text{ cm}$ cavity floor, $0.5\text{ cm}$ step size) in Three.js with a translucent cyan slice plane and glowing bounding perimeter edge.
   - Dynamically compute volumetric cavity fill ($V(d_{cut}) = V_0 \times (\max(0, \frac{d_{max} - d_{cut}}{d_{max}}))^{1.5}$ Liters), dense bituminous macadam mass ($M_{asphalt} = V(d_{cut}) \times 2.3\text{ kg/L}$), and PWD SOR 2025 repair cost ($\text{Cost}(d_{cut}) = \text{costInr} \times \frac{V(d_{cut})}{V_0}$) below the cut-plane threshold in real-time.
   - Implement elevation contour heatmaps with high-contrast vertex coloring: Emerald Green ($0\text{ cm}$ baseline), Goldenrod Amber ($-1\text{ cm}$ to $-5\text{ cm}$ rutting/shear), and Crimson Red ($> 5\text{ cm}$ critical cavity void).
   - Provide 3-way mesh display modes (`solid` Textured Solid, `wireframe` Wireframe Matrix, `contour` Elevation Contour Heatmap) and camera inspection modes (`turntable` Orbit Turntable view vs `walk` Driver Walk POV view).

3. **4-Channel Multi-Camera Exterior Perspective Switcher (TWIN-02, D-16-03)**:
   - Provide a synchronized 4-camera exterior perspective dock covering transit bus monitoring angles:
     1. `CAM-01 • Front Windshield Dashcam` (Primary optical hazard detection)
     2. `CAM-02 • Rear Road Surface Overview` (Trailing rear axle impact & trailing distress)
     3. `CAM-03 • Left Curb / Footpath View` (Curb subsidence, sidewalk clearance, open manholes)
     4. `CAM-04 • Right Carriageway / Overtaking Lane` (Dividers, lane encroachment, traffic flow)
   - Render a standardized HUD telemetry strip for each camera perspective: channel code & label, GPS timestamp, vehicle speed ($v_{km/h}$), speed-normalized shock ($G_{norm}$), bus ID, and GPS coordinates.

4. **Dual Access Entry Points with (Concept) Badging (TWIN-01, TWIN-02, D-16-04)**:
   - WebGIS Map cluster popup cards in `WebGISMap.tsx` render an explicit CTA button: `⚠️ Inspect 3D Surface Mesh (Concept) →`, wired via `window.__roadsaathi_open_mesh_modal` in `CommandCenter.tsx`.
   - `OperatorInspectorPanel.tsx` triage dock renders an explicit `Inspect 3D Surface Mesh (Concept)` action trigger.
   - `WorkOrders.tsx` CAD Backlog table row renders an explicit `(Concept)` badge pill and tooltip on the 3D mesh trigger.
   - `WorkOrderActionConsole.tsx` CAD Audit tab renders an `Inspect 3D Corridor Mesh (Concept)` action button, seamlessly opening `RoadMeshVisualizerModal.tsx` pre-bound to defect telemetry.

### Threat Model
- **T-16-01: Statutory Misrepresentation of Exploratory 3D Mesh as Survey-Grade LiDAR**:
  - *Risk*: PWD contractors facing liquidated damages under IRC:SP:20 Clause 14 could challenge penalty assessments in arbitration if 3D cavity models are misconstrued as physical millimeter-accurate terrestrial LiDAR scans.
  - *Mitigation*: Enforce mandatory constraint D-16-01 across all UI headers, inspection modal banners, button badges, tooltips, and documentation. Non-dismissible amber/indigo banner in `RoadMeshVisualizerModal.tsx` explicitly designates the 3D surface elevation mesh as an *"Experimental Concept / Proof-of-Concept Prototype synthesized from monocular dashcam depth + IMU elevation profile (Non-LiDAR exploratory prototype)"*.
- **T-16-02: WebGL Context & GPU Resource Leaks on Repeated Modal Mounts**:
  - *Risk*: Repeatedly opening and closing the 3D visualizer modal across multiple road defects during high-throughput triage can exhaust WebGL contexts (browsers enforce a ceiling of 8–16 active contexts) and crash the tab.
  - *Mitigation*: Strict Three.js cleanup lifecycle in `useEffect` unmount: recursively traverse scene, dispose geometry (`geometry.dispose()`), dispose materials (`material.dispose()`), dispose textures, call `renderer.forceContextLoss()`, `renderer.dispose()`, and cancel `requestAnimationFrame` animation loops cleanly.
- **T-16-03: Cut-Plane Slider React Re-rendering & WebGL Bottlenecks**:
  - *Risk*: Rapid dragging of the cut-plane depth slider (0 to 25 cm with 0.5 cm steps) triggering full Three.js scene rebuilding or expensive re-renders that drop frames below 60 FPS.
  - *Mitigation*: Decouple cut-plane mesh translation: update the Three.js cut-plane mesh `position.y` directly via reference or memoized update without disposing/rebuilding the 9,216-vertex `PlaneGeometry`. Compute cavity volume formula $V(d_{cut}) = V_0 \times (\max(0, \frac{d_{max} - d_{cut}}{d_{max}}))^{1.5}$ and PWD SOR repair cost efficiently in pure JavaScript without DOM layout thrashing.
</planning_context>
