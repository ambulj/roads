# Plan 13-01: Municipal Statutory Show-Cause Notice Pack, Dynamic DLP Windows, Trip-Windowed Concurrence, and Civic UI Modernization

<planning_context>
### Objective
Transition RoadSaathi's contractor liability enforcement and post-repair clearance mechanisms from legally vulnerable automated actions into an incontestable, legally compliant municipal statutory workflow in accordance with Milestone `v4.0`, Indian contract and arbitration statutes, Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam 2023 (BSA 2023), and Requirements `STATUTE-01` and `STATUTE-02`:
1. **Statutory Form 14 Show-Cause Evidentiary Pack (STATUTE-01)**:
   - Replace unilateral automated bank guarantee encashment ("Auto-Debit") with a Two-Stage Section 63/65B BSA 2023 Statutory Show-Cause Notice workflow under IRC:SP:20 Clause 14.2 and MoRTH Section 3000.
   - Stage 1: Autonomous drafting of Form 14 evidentiary pack with SHA-256 cryptographic provenance hash, 3-frame visual comparison (Pre-Repair / Post-Compaction / Recurrent Distress), tiered penalty assessment (₹25k 1st, ₹50k 2nd, ₹100k + GeM debarment alert 3rd+), and 7-day cure window countdown clock. State transitions to `UNDER_NOTICE_CURE_PERIOD` (disputed holdback, not liquidated).
   - Stage 2: Municipal Executive Engineer (EE) digital sign-off generating an admissible Section 63/65B BSA Certificate of Electronic Records, transitioning state to `NOTICE_SERVED`.
   - Post-Notice Enforcement: If unrectified after 7 days, state transitions to `LIQUIDATED_DAMAGES_ENFORCED` and records CAG-compliant PFMS transaction vouchers. If rectified and multi-pass verified within 7 days, state transitions to `CURED_BY_CONTRACTOR` (holdback released without penalty).
2. **Contract Package Defect Liability Period (DLP) Tiers (STATUTE-01)**:
   - Dynamic boundary arithmetic supporting standard contract types:
     - Bituminous Overlay (Routine Maintenance): 12 months (365 days).
     - Flexible Arterial (DBM + BC - Default Standard): 36 months (1,095 days).
     - Rigid Concrete / PQC / White-topping: 60 months (1,825 days).
   - Evaluate boundary conditions in `evaluate_recurrence_window(resolved_at, detected_at, dlp_months)`.
3. **Trip-Windowed Multi-Pass Concurrence & Optical Clearance Dominance (STATUTE-02)**:
   - Relax the strict 3-distinct-bus rule to 3 distinct patrol passes within 48 hours. A pass is valid if from a distinct vehicle ID OR from the same vehicle on distinct trips separated by at least 2 hours ($\Delta t \ge 7,200\text{ seconds}$), eliminating single-bus corridor starvation (e.g., MTC Route 570 or 19B).
   - Optical Clearance Dominance: Windshield camera confirming `optical_status == "SMOOTH_SURFACE"` (or confidence $< 0.10$ without cavity) qualifies the pass as verified even if defensive steering or lane straddling prevented direct wheel collision.
   - Mechanical Shock Override: Speed-normalized shock $G_{norm} \ge 0.35$ immediately overrides optical smoothness and triggers `REPAIR_FAILED_RECURRENCE`.
4. **Civic UI Modernization & Terminology Alignment (STATUTE-01, STATUTE-02)**:
   - Replace all "Auto-Debit" strings with statutory show-cause notice terminology across `ContractorSlaLedger.tsx`, `WorkOrders.tsx`, and `WorkOrderActionConsole.tsx`.
   - Introduce Form 14 Show-Cause Notice Pack Modal with 7-day cure countdown, before/after keyframes, Section 65B BSA electronic certificate seal, and EE digital signature button.
   - Update Work Order Action Console Tab 3 with trip-windowed pass breakdown and optical clearance dominance indicators.

### Threat Model
- **T-13-01: Premature Unilateral Bank Guarantee Encashment Injunctions**:
  - *Risk*: Arbitral tribunals under Section 9 of the Arbitration & Conciliation Act 1996 and civil courts grant immediate injunctions against municipal bank guarantee encashment if executed unilaterally without prior notice and reasonable opportunity to cure.
  - *Mitigation*: The system introduces a Two-Stage State Machine: defect detection stages a statutory Form 14 Show-Cause Notice in `UNDER_NOTICE_CURE_PERIOD` (disputed holdback, not liquidated). Escrow funds remain held but not liquidated until Executive Engineer digital sign-off (`NOTICE_SERVED`) and the mandatory 7-day cure window expires without contractor rectification (`LIQUIDATED_DAMAGES_ENFORCED`).
- **T-13-02: Single-Corridor Starvation vs Sybil Rapid Re-Passes**:
  - *Risk*: Permitting the same vehicle ID to contribute multiple passes could allow a fraudulent actor to circle a block in 5 minutes to forge 3-pass consensus. Conversely, requiring distinct vehicle IDs starves single-bus corridors (MTC 570 or 19B).
  - *Mitigation*: The trip-windowed algorithm strictly requires $\Delta t \ge 7,200\text{ seconds}$ (2 hours) between passes from the same vehicle ID within the 48-hour monitoring window. Passes within 2 hours are deduplicated to 1 qualifying pass.
- **T-13-03: Section 65B BSA Admissibility Challenge (Evidence Tampering)**:
  - *Risk*: Contractors contest electronic telemetry and camera records in court alleging digital manipulation or lack of device certificate.
  - *Mitigation*: Every Form 14 pack includes an immutable SHA-256 digest computed across `cin:cluster_code:penalty_amount:timestamp:bus_id:measured_gz`, linked to a formal Section 63/65B BSA certificate ID (`CERT-BSA63-...`), verifying device custody and operational regularity under Indian evidence law.

       ```python
       def evaluate_recurrence_window(
           resolved_at_str: Union[str, datetime.datetime], 
           detected_at_str: Union[str, datetime.datetime],
           dlp_months: int = 36
       ) -> bool:
           """
           Evaluates whether a detected defect falls within the Defect Liability Period (DLP).
           Supports dynamic statutory tiers:
             - 12 months (Bituminous Overlay): 365 days
             - 36 months (Flexible Arterial - default): 1,095 days
             - 60 months (Rigid Concrete / PQC): 1,825 days
           """
           t_resolved = _parse_datetime(resolved_at_str)
           t_detected = _parse_datetime(detected_at_str)

           if t_resolved.tzinfo is not None and t_detected.tzinfo is None:
               t_detected = t_detected.replace(tzinfo=t_resolved.tzinfo)
           elif t_resolved.tzinfo is None and t_detected.tzinfo is not None:
               t_resolved = t_resolved.replace(tzinfo=t_detected.tzinfo)

           delta = t_detected - t_resolved
           total_seconds = delta.total_seconds()
           
           days_map = {12: 365, 36: 1095, 60: 1825}
           allowed_days = days_map.get(dlp_months, int(dlp_months * 365.25 / 12))
           
           return 0 <= total_seconds <= (allowed_days * 86400)
       ```
