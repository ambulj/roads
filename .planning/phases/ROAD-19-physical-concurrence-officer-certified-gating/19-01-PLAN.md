<planning_context>
### Objective
Ground road repair verification and municipal contractor accountability in empirical physics and statutory governance:
1. **Time-Windowed Post-Repair Verification (D-19-01, GATE-01)**: Filter telematics strictly to post-repair passes where $t \in [t_{\text{repair}},\, t_{\text{repair}} + 48\text{h}]$, ignoring pre-repair defect detections that previously triggered false recurrence penalties on compliant contractors.
2. **Corridor Tolerance & Speed-Normalized Shock Jerk (D-19-02, GATE-01)**: Narrow the spatial matching corridor from 15.0m (30m circle) to 5.0m lane-aligned tolerance, preventing adjacent utility trenches and speed breakers from contaminating repair audits. Normalize vertical shock via $G_{norm} = \frac{|G_z - 1.0|}{v / 30.0}$.
3. **Fail-Closed Missing Sensor Handling & Zero Fallback Fabrication (D-19-02, D-19-03, GATE-02)**: Telematics missing vertical acceleration ($G_z$) or camera optical status, or vehicles crawling at $v < 15\text{ km/h}$, fail closed as `NO_DATA` (never counting towards the 3 clean passes required for payment release, nor falsely failing repairs). Completely eliminate synthetic fallback penalty values ($1.48\text{g}$, `"BUS-04"`).
4. **Officer-Certified Recommendation Workflows & Structured Defect Triage (D-19-04, D-19-05, OFFICER-01)**: Replace unilateral autonomous escrow debits (`execute_contractor_auto_debit`) with structured statutory recommendations (`recommend_release`, `proposed_deduction`, `approval_status`) requiring Assistant Engineer (AE) / Executive Engineer (EE) digital sign-off. Extend `DBDistressCluster` with `repair_completed_at`, `acknowledged_at`, `assigned_officer_id`, `rejection_reason` (constrained to `FALSE_POSITIVE`, `DUPLICATE_CLUSTER`, `JURISDICTION_NON_PWD`, `UNDER_ACTIVE_REPAIR`), and `triage_status`.

### Scope Boundaries
- **In-Scope**:
  - Telematics windowing ($[t_{\text{repair}}, t_{\text{repair}} + 48\text{h}]$) in `concurrence_service.py` across SQLite and PostgreSQL/PostGIS.
  - Narrowing `CORRIDOR_MATCH_METERS` from 15.0 to 5.0 in `concurrence_service.py`.
  - Fail-closed handling for missing sensors and $v < 15\text{ km/h}$ crawling vehicles.
  - Complete elimination of hardcoded $1.48\text{g}$ and `"BUS-04"` defaults in `concurrence_service.py` and `contractor_service.py`.
  - Officer recommendation data models in `schemas.py`, `concurrence_service.py`, and `WorkOrderActionConsole.tsx`.
  - Defect triage fields on `DBDistressCluster`, SQLite PRAGMA table migration in `database.py`, and `POST /api/clusters/{id}/triage` endpoint.
- **Out-of-Scope (Deferred to Phase 20/21)**:
  - Contractual DLP clause numbering rectification and PFMS escrow voucher terminology renames (Phase 20).
  - Rectangular saw-cut box trim (+15%) and asphalt compaction density adjustments (Phase 20).
  - Neural network weights metric honesty table and IRC:106 PCU urban factors (Phase 21).

### Threat Model
- **T-19-01 (Pre-Repair Telematics Recurrence False Positive)**: Unbounded temporal queries match pre-repair defect telemetry (high $G_z$, open cavity) captured before repair work was completed, falsely transitioning repaired work orders to `REPAIR_FAILED_RECURRENCE` and levying unearned ₹25k–₹100k penalties. *Mitigation*: Strictly enforce $t \in [t_{\text{repair}}, t_{\text{repair}} + 48\text{h}]$, completely ignoring pre-repair captures.
- **T-19-02 (Missing Sensor Default Bypass & Crawl Speed Under-Deflection)**: Unequipped fleet buses missing IMU or optical sensors defaulting to clean passes ($1.0\text{g}$, `SMOOTH_SURFACE`) allow unverified repairs to be improperly released. Buses crawling below $15\text{ km/h}$ lack suspension deflection. *Mitigation*: Any pass missing $G_z$, missing optical status, or traveling at $v < 15\text{ km/h}$ is flagged `is_no_data = True` and fail-closed as `NO_DATA`. It never counts toward the 3 verified passes.
- **T-19-03 (Unilateral Autonomous Escrow Liquidations & Evidence Fabrication)**: Unilateral automated escrow debits without municipal engineer digital certification violate Indian arbitration norms and Section 65B BSA 2023 evidentiary standards. Hardcoding synthetic defaults ($1.48\text{g}$, `"BUS-04"`) exposes municipal records to judicial dispute. *Mitigation*: Eliminate all synthetic values; only empirical tripping passes propose deductions; convert autonomous deductions into statutory recommendations (`recommend_release`, `proposed_deduction`, AE/EE approval).
