<planning_context>
### Objective
Ground vehicle license plate recognition in empirical AI reality:
1. Wire the fine-tuned character detector (`backend/app/weights/anpr.pt`).
2. Completely remove all synthetic plate generation based on pixel coordinate modulo hashing (`rto_idx = (vx1*13 + vy1*7) % len(rto_list)`), artificial `0.94`/`0.95` confidence floors, and fallback `"TN01AB1234"` stubs.
3. Enforce strict review queue routing: plates with confidence $< 0.90$ or unreadable crops route to `REQUIRES_HUMAN_REVIEW` rather than receiving automatic admissibility.
4. Standardize 100% of codebase references and localStorage keys to **RoadSaathi**, removing legacy `SeherSaathi` references.

### Threat Model
- **T-18-01 (Plate Fabrication Liability)**: Fabricating vehicle registration numbers falsely accuses innocent vehicle owners in hit-and-run incidents. Mitigated by failing-closed when no characters are resolved.
- **T-18-02 (Review Queue Starvation)**: Artificial confidence floors ($\ge 0.94$) bypass human oversight. Mitigated by reporting genuine arithmetic mean confidence.
- **T-18-03 (Session Loss on Rebrand)**: Changing localStorage keys could log out active operators. Mitigated by fallback migration in auth context.
</planning_context>
