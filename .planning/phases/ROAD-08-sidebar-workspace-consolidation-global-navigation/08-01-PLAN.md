# 08-01-PLAN: Sidebar 5-Workspace Consolidation, Sub-Tab Switchers & Mobile Navigation Polish

<planning_context>
### Objective
Consolidate RoadSaathi's multi-tier navigation hierarchy into **5 high-cohesion municipal workspaces** (Command Center, Work Orders & SLA, Safety & Traffic Analytics, Fleet Perception & Edge, Governance & Memory). Replace scattered navigation items with tokenized in-page indigo sub-tab switchers, modern responsive desktop collapse tooltips, unified keyboard shortcuts (`1`-`5`), backward-compatible route aliasing, and a streamlined mobile bottom navigation bar with a "More" drawer.

### Scope Boundaries
- **In-Scope**:
  1. Consolidating `SidebarNav.tsx` to exactly 5 workspace items with live counter badges, smooth collapse hover tooltips, and active indigo styling.
  2. Updating `App.tsx` and `Header.tsx` routing definitions, digit shortcut bindings (`1`-`5`), and canonical URL redirect handlers for legacy bookmarks (`#/incidents` $\to$ `#/analytics?tab=incidents`, `#/capture` $\to$ `#/fleet?tab=capture`).
  3. Standardizing workspace header sub-tab switchers across `WorkOrders.tsx` (`[CAD Backlog] [Contractor SLA Ledger]`), `FleetNodes.tsx` (`[Fleet Nodes] [Dashcam Ingest]`), and `RoadMemory.tsx` (`[Road Intelligence] [Markov Deterioration & MLOps] [MoHUA Audit Trail]`).
  4. Redesigning `BottomNav.tsx` for mobile viewports ($< 768\text{px}$) with 4 primary touch targets and a slide-over "More" drawer for Governance and system tools.
- **Out-of-Scope**:
  - Full redesign of WebGIS map viewport overlays or bottom trays (handled in Phase 9).
  - Full tabular redesign of executive analytics and compact backlog cell layout (handled in Phase 10).

### Threat Model & Risk Mitigations
- **T-08-01 (Route Regression)**: Bookmarked URLs (`#/incidents`, `#/capture`) breaking or returning blank views.
  *Mitigation*: Implement automatic alias resolution in `App.tsx` `handleNavigate` and `getInitialRoute` that maps legacy paths to parent routes with query parameters (`?tab=...`).
- **T-08-02 (Persona Clearance Lockout)**: Restricted roles (e.g. `traffic_police`, `rto_officer`, `pwd_engineer`) losing access to sub-features due to route consolidation.
  *Mitigation*: Align `AuthContext.tsx` and `hasAccessToRoute` checks to validate workspace access while allowing fine-grained sub-tab permissions.
- **T-08-03 (Contrast & Accessibility Regression)**: Low contrast active tab indicators in dark or light mode failing WCAG AAA ($< 7:1$).
  *Mitigation*: Use verified high-contrast styling tokens (`bg-indigo-50 dark:bg-indigo-950/60 text-indigo-700 dark:text-indigo-300 border-indigo-200 dark:border-indigo-800/80`).
</planning_context>
