# Plan 04-02: PS 26124 Origin-Destination Transit Desire Lines Layer & Traffic Analytics

<planning_context>
### Objective
Implement and verify the complete Origin-Destination (OD) Transit Desire Lines geospatial analytics pipeline fulfilling Problem Statement **PS 26124**. Connect major Chennai multimodal transit corridors with curvature-interpolated GeoJSON Great Circle/Bezier desire lines on MapLibre GL, encoding daily passenger volumes, Level of Service (LoS), and pavement distress (IRI) ridership delay penalties:
1. **Backend Traffic OD Analytics Engine (`backend/app/api/endpoints/traffic.py`)**: Serve structured origin-destination flow matrices (`GET /api/traffic/od-matrix`), corridor traffic density records (`GET /api/traffic/density`), and active bottleneck alerts (`GET /api/traffic/bottlenecks`) calculating Passenger Car Units (PCU per IRC:106), peak travel-time ratios (TTR), and surface distress delay attributions.
2. **Interactive MapLibre GL Desire Lines Layer (`frontend/src/components/map/WebGISMap.tsx`)**: Render dynamic curved GeoJSON transit desire lines connecting key regional hubs (Chennai Central, Guindy Intermodal, Tambaram Junction, OMR Tidel Park, Kathipara Cloverleaf, CMBT Koyambedu), with volume-weighted stroke widths ($3.5 - 5.5\text{px}$), passenger density color ramps ($<10\text{k}$ cyan, $10\text{k}-25\text{k}$ blue/purple, $>25\text{k}$ amber/rose), interactive hover tooltips, ridership roughness delay cards, and dedicated map layer toggle controls.

### Scope Boundaries
- **In Scope:**
  - Backend OD Matrix and Traffic endpoints in `backend/app/api/endpoints/traffic.py`:
    - `GET /api/traffic/od-matrix`: Returns validated OD corridor pairs with PCU flow, scheduled vs actual transit time, distress delay minutes, and Level of Service (LoS).
    - `GET /api/traffic/density`: Returns PCU/km density, average speed, and congestion classification across Chennai arterial trunks.
    - `GET /api/traffic/bottlenecks`: Returns active carriageway constrictions and AI transit diversion advisories.
  - Test coverage in `backend/tests/test_explainable_webgis.py` verifying response schema, non-negative PCU values, and corridor definitions.
  - Frontend API service client integration in `frontend/src/services/api.ts`:
    - `getODMatrix()`, `getTrafficDensity()`, `getBottlenecks()`.
  - GeoJSON curvature interpolation and layer rendering in `frontend/src/components/map/WebGISMap.tsx`:
    - `od-desire-lines-src` and `od-desire-lines-layer` in MapLibre GL.
    - Interactive click/hover popups with trips/day, cabin load proxy, distress delay, and LoS ratings.
    - Layer toggle control in the WebGIS layer panel (`showODDesireLines`).
- **Out of Scope:**
  - RPI formula engine and 30 FPS video perception canvas (Plan 04-01).
  - Physical GTFS static file parser background cron daemon (uses GTFS Analytics Engine simulation).

### Threat Model
- **T-04-02 (WebGL Map Context Loss on Re-renders)**: Frequently adding and removing MapLibre layers or GeoJSON sources causes WebGL buffer reallocation and context loss. Mitigated by checking `map.getSource()` and invoking `(source as GeoJSONSource).setData()` to update geometries reactively without tearing down layer pipelines.
- **T-04-04 (Network Offline in Field Demonstrations)**: High-latency or disconnected demo environments could fail to fetch live OD matrices. Mitigated by embedding high-fidelity fallback GeoJSON seed datasets (`OD_DESIRE_LINES_GEOJSON`) within `WebGISMap.tsx` and resilient error fallbacks in `api.ts`.
</planning_context>
