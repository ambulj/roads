# Plan 04-01: Interactive RPI Mathematical Explainer & Live Dashcam 30 FPS Perception Visualizer

<planning_context>
### Objective
Deliver the explainable Road Priority Index (RPI) mathematical engine and live dashcam 30 FPS computer vision perception visualizer. Provide municipal engineers, judicial review panels, and civic evaluators with complete, interactive transparency into how road distress is prioritized and verified:
1. **Interactive RPI Explainer (`GIS-01`)**: An explainable RPI calculation engine and UI modal (`RPIFormulaModal.tsx`) that breaks down statutory weights ($40\%$ Defect Severity, $20\%$ Multi-Bus Consensus Logarithmic Scaling, $20\%$ Road Hierarchy, $20\%$ Sensitive POI Proximity, and IMD Monsoon Multiplier $1.0 - 1.30\times$), supporting real-time slider parameter tuning, deterministic SLA re-evaluation (P0 $<24\text{h}$, P1 $<48\text{h}$, P2 $<72\text{h}$), and dynamic pre-population from clicked MapLibre defect cluster pins.
2. **Live Dashcam 30 FPS Perception Visualizer (`GIS-02`)**: A synchronized dual-pane video perception modal (`UploadFootageModal.tsx`) executing a zero-latency `requestAnimationFrame` Canvas overlay at 30 FPS. Renders color-coded tracking bounding boxes, confidence tags, DPDP Act 2023 bystander/plate anonymization masks, 3D pothole depth wireframe grids with cylindrical cavity volume estimation ($V = \frac{\pi}{4} \cdot d^2 \cdot h$), and a 5Hz synchronized AIS-140 IMU/GPS telemetry ticker across 4 pre-bundled offline demonstration scenarios.

### Scope Boundaries
- **In Scope:**
  - Backend statutory RPI formula synchronization in `backend/app/core/rpi_engine.py`:
    $$\text{RPI} = \min\left(100.0, \left(0.40 \cdot S + 0.20 \cdot \min\left(100, 20 \cdot \log_2(1 + N)\right) + 0.20 \cdot W_{\text{road}} + 0.20 \cdot \max\left(0, 100 \left(1 - \frac{D_{\text{poi}}}{1500}\right)\right)\right) \cdot M_{\text{monsoon}}\right)$$
  - Comprehensive unit test suite in `backend/tests/test_explainable_webgis.py` covering formula weights, boundary clamping, logarithmic scaling steps, SLA classification thresholds, and pothole cavity volume formulas.
  - Interactive slider controls and live equation breakdown card in `frontend/src/components/modals/RPIFormulaModal.tsx` bound to active cluster data.
  - Client-side 30 FPS Canvas HUD rendering loop with `requestAnimationFrame` in `frontend/src/components/modals/UploadFootageModal.tsx`.
  - Pothole 3D wireframe mesh grid overlay and cavity repair volume calculation ($V = \frac{\pi}{4} d^2 h$).
  - Synchronized 5Hz telemetry HUD ticker displaying vehicle speed, vertical shock acceleration ($g_z$), and optical match probabilities.
  - 4 pre-bundled offline demonstration clips in `frontend/public/sample_clips/` for instant field presentations.
- **Out of Scope:**
  - Origin-Destination Transit Desire Lines (PS 26124) MapLibre layer and backend `/api/traffic/od-matrix` endpoints (Plan 04-02).
  - Contractor Defect Liability auto-debit ledger and work order concurrence gates (Phase 3).

### Threat Model
- **T-04-01 (Large Video Client OOM / Crash)**: Uploading high-resolution raw video files could exhaust browser memory or cause tab crashes. Mitigated by enforcing a 100MB file size cap, strict MIME validation (`video/mp4`, `video/webm`), and providing pre-bundled optimized demonstration clips with instant fallback.
- **T-04-03 (Formula Boundary Violations & NaN)**: Extreme slider inputs or negative distances could lead to mathematical anomalies or scores $> 100$. Mitigated by strictly clamping all term inputs ($S \in [0, 100]$, $N \in [1, 50]$, $W \in [0, 100]$, $D \in [0, 1500]$, $M \in [1.0, 1.30]$) and wrapping total sums in `Math.min(100.0, Math.max(0.0, ...))`.
</planning_context>
